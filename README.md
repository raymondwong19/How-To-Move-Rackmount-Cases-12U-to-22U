# 🚚 Safely Moving a Small-to-Medium Rack Case (12U - 22U) Guide (Bot generated with comments from me)

Welcome to the definitive guide for safely relocating your 12U to 22U rackmount case.

**⚠️ SAFETY FIRST:** Always prioritize proper lifting techniques - lift with your legs like crouch and try to stand back up again. For heavier cases (especially 22U or those still partially populated), use a second person, or hug it really tight (since they're man sized).

---

## 📋 Pre-Move Checklist

Before you begin, ensure you have the following:

*   **Tools:**
    *   Pliers or wire cutters (to cut old zip ties)
    *   New high-quality zip ties (for re-securing internal components)
    *   Label maker or masking tape and a marker
    *   ESD-safe wrist strap and mat (highly recommended)
*   **A safe surface:** A sturdy wooden table or a grounded workbench to temporarily place components.
*   **Clear path:** Ensure a clear route to the new location.

---

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Moving Procedure

Follow these steps in order to ensure the survival of both you and your equipment.

### Phase 1: Preparation and Disassembly

**Step 1: Electrostatic Discharge (ESD) Prevention & General Safety**

*   Avoid building up static electricity. Wear shoes (rubber soles help).
*   Avoid working on carpeted areas if possible. Also NEVER drag your feet nor your case.
*   **Always** ground yourself before touching metal components. The best practice is to wear a grounded ESD wrist strap connected to a grounded metal object or an ESD mat.
*   **I will just touch a doorknob all the time or my metal grocery shelf. Standard survival protocol: When moving between areas, quickly touch a doorknob or another grounded object to discharge static.**

**Step 2: Disconnect and Label External Cables**

*   **CRITICAL:** Label *every single cable* on both ends before unplugging it. This includes network cables, peripheral cables, and power cords. Well I mean you SHOULD have done it beforehand during first setup and adding of equipment.
*   Unplug all external cables leading into the case, including the main power cord(s).
*   Shut down any exposed batteries or external power backup units.
*   **Wait:** After disconnecting power, wait at least **1 minute** (some manufacturers recommend 5-15 minutes for large PSUs) to allow power supply unit (PSU) capacitors to safely discharge their residual power.

**Step 3: Remove Side Doors for Access and Weight Reduction**

*   Unlock and remove the side doors of the rack case. This lessens the weight and gives you better handles for carrying the case later.
*   Lean the doors safely against a wall, out of the way.

**Step 4: Remove Heavy Internal Components**

*   All heavy occupants **must** be removed to prevent damage during movement.
*   Identify heavy items: GPU cards, large power supplies, heavy server chassis/shelves. Motherboards are typically lightweight and can remain on their shelf.
Also use your cutters to cut the zip tie seatbelt that holds the heavy PSUs and GPUs to their shelf obviously so they can be removed before the shelf itself.

**Step 5: Unscrew and Remove Rack Shelves/Chassis**

*   Unscrew all rack-mounted components (shelves, blanking panels, motherboard trays). They are usually held by four handscrews each.
*   Start unscrewing and removing from the **bottom to the top**. Provide support when removing each unit.
*   Place these lighter components and screws on your safe work surface.

### Phase 2: The Move

**Step 6: Move Components to the New Location**

*   Move the removed components (GPU cards, PSUs, shelves, doors) one by one to the new location. Ensure the new location also has a safe surface ready.

**Step 7: Move the Empty Rack Case**

*   The case itself is now as light as it will get, but still heavy.
*   **Do not drag the case.** Always heave/lift it to avoid damaging the case feet or the floor/carpet, which can also build up static.
*   **Lifting Technique:**
    *   **12U-15U:** You may need to carry it hunched over slightly.
    *   **22U:** You will likely need to "hug" the case vertically to get a good grip and use your leg strength more effectively.
*   Move the case to its final destination.

### Phase 3: Reassembly

**Step 8: Set Up the Case**

*   Place the empty case securely in its final spot.

**Step 9: Reinstall Rack Shelves/Chassis**

*   Screw the empty or still with motherboard seated rack-mounted shelves/trays back into the case.
*   Work from the **top shelf down to the bottom**, which helps maintain structural integrity and alignment during reassembly. Use all four handscrews per unit.

**Step 10: Reinstall Components and Secure Them (NEW STEP ADDED)**

*   Now that the shelves are securely screwed into the case, place your heavy components (GPUs, PSUs, etc.) back onto their respective shelves/slots.
*   **Secure everything:** Use *new* zip ties as "seatbelts" and re-attach all retention screws. Ensure every single component is firmly secured and cannot move. This prevents damage from vibration or accidental bumps later.

**Step 11: Reconnect Internal Cables**

*   Reconnect any internal power or data cables that span between different shelves or components using your labels. Ensure cables are neatly managed and not blocking airflow.

**Step 12: Reattach and Position Doors**

*   Reattach the side doors.
*   Depending on your airflow needs, open them or leave them shut.

**Step 13: External Cabling and Power Up**

*   Refer to your labels and reconnect all external cables. Double-check all connections before powering up.

**Step 14: Final Check**

*   Power on your equipment and verify that everything is running correctly.

## 🛑 Summary of "Don'ts"

*   ❌ **Don't** drag the case on the ground.
*   ❌ **Don't** move the case with heavy components still inside.
*   ❌ **Don't** handle internal components without grounding yourself first.
*   ❌ **Don't** reuse old, stressed zip ties for safety restraints.
*   ❌ **Don't** lift with your back.
